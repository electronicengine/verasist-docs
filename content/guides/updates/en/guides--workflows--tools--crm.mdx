<p>The CRM tool lets your AI agent manage contacts in your organization's connected CRM — search for an existing contact, create a new one, update one, or list recent contacts — all during a live conversation.</p>
<blockquote class="note">This tool acts through a CRM account you've already connected under <strong>Settings → CRM</strong>. Connect the account first, then create the CRM tool and point it at that account.</blockquote>

<h2>Connecting an Account</h2>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Settings → CRM</strong> and choose <strong>Connect Account</strong></li>
<li>Select a provider (HubSpot or Dynamics 365)</li>
<li>For Dynamics 365, enter your <strong>Tenant ID</strong> and <strong>Instance URL</strong> (e.g. <code>https://yourorg.crm.dynamics.com</code>); HubSpot needs no extra fields</li>
<li>Complete the OAuth consent flow in the popup/redirect</li>
<li>The connected account appears in the CRM tool's account picker when you configure the tool</li>
</ol>
Verasist automatically refreshes the OAuth access token when it expires, so long-running agent sessions keep working without re-authentication.

<h2>Configuration</h2>
<h3>Basic Settings</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>CRM Account</strong>: The connected HubSpot or Dynamics 365 account this tool uses</li>
<li><strong>Allow Search</strong>: Let the agent search for a contact by email or phone (default: on)</li>
<li><strong>Allow Create</strong>: Let the agent create a new contact (default: on)</li>
<li><strong>Allow Update</strong>: Let the agent update an existing contact (default: on)</li>
<li><strong>Allow List</strong>: Let the agent list recent contacts (default: on)</li>
<li><strong>Custom Message</strong>: Optional message played to the caller before the tool executes</li>
</ul>
At least one capability (search, create, update, or list) must be enabled.
<h2>Agent Usage</h2>
<h3>Search for a Contact</h3>
<pre><code class="language-">crm_search_contact(email="jane@example.com")
</code></pre>
<h3>Create a Contact</h3>
<pre><code class="language-">crm_create_contact(first_name="Jane", last_name="Doe", email="jane@example.com", phone="+14155552671", company="Acme Inc.")
</code></pre>
<h3>Update a Contact</h3>
<pre><code class="language-">crm_update_contact(record_id="12345", phone="+14155552672")
</code></pre>
<code>record_id</code> comes from a prior search, create, or list call.
<h3>List Recent Contacts</h3>
<pre><code class="language-">crm_list_contacts(limit=10)
</code></pre>
<p>Common scenarios:
<ul>
<li>Checking whether a caller already exists in the CRM before creating a duplicate</li>
<li>Logging a new lead captured during the call</li>
<li>Updating a contact's phone number or job title after the caller confirms it</li>
<li>Pulling up recently added contacts for a sales follow-up</li>
</ul>
<blockquote class="note">The tool fails closed if the configured CRM account doesn't belong to your organization, is inactive, or its OAuth token can't be refreshed — the agent receives a clear error and the conversation continues instead of failing the call.</blockquote></p><h2>Current setup and execution</h2><p>CRM: configure crm connection and enabled contact operations, attach the saved tool to an agent, and test its operation in a workflow.</p><ul><li>Enabled operations and account access determine what the agent can do.
